# Focus Session: Learning in Virtual & Extended Reality (VR/AR/XR)

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Scope:** Deep-dive into VR/AR/MR/XR/immersive learning within the learning-research corpus  
**Corpus:** 29,592 papers (21 categories, 9 aspects)  
**Matched:** 871 VR/AR/XR/immersive papers (2.94% of corpus)  
**Learning-oriented subset:** 468 papers

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Virtual and Extended Reality is a **rapidly emerging learning paradigm** within the corpus, growing **5× in three years** (31 papers in 2022 → 159 in 2025). It spans cognitive science, education, motor learning, and accessibility. The evidence base is **theory-heavy** (40% theory, 27% method) with strong threads in **immersive training/skill acquisition**, **assessment via multimodal data**, and **accessibility for diverse learners**.

**Key finding:** VR/XR is not a single paradigm but a **convergence layer** — it appears across 10 of 21 categories, acting as an *interface modality* that amplifies embodied, immersive, and collaborative learning. Its strongest research concentration is in **cognitive-science** (142 papers, 5.8% of that category) and **education** (88 papers, 6.2%), with a notably high share in **motor learning** (60 papers, 7.7%).

## 🔗 Implications for ki-kompetenz-training & HPC Courses

### For ki-kompetenz-training (AI Literacy)

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| AR (28%) is strong in education | AR overlays could contextualize AI literacy prompts at the point of work |
| Accessibility thread (71 papers) | VR/AR can make AI literacy accessible to diverse learners (deaf, visually impaired) |
| Assessment via multimodal data (237) | VR could enable richer AI literacy assessment (gesture, gaze, interaction) |
| Immersive social interaction + LLM (2026) | VR + LLM humanoids for AI literacy roleplay scenarios |

**Concrete opportunity:** AR-assisted AI literacy — overlaying transparency/disclosure prompts onto real AI tool usage. The 2026 "Affective Tools for Thought" paper suggests VR can scaffold metacognitive awareness, directly relevant to AI literacy's "critical thinking about AI" objective.

### For HPC Courses

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| Motor learning has highest VR penetration (7.7%) | VR is natural for HPC operational training (cluster operations, hardware) |
| Virtual training/simulation (69 papers) | Simulated HPC environments (safe practice on virtual clusters) |
| Embodied learning (145 papers) | Physical interaction metaphors for parallel computing concepts |
| Assessment with multimodal data | VR can capture gaze/interaction to assess HPC skill acquisition |

**Concrete opportunity:** VR-based HPC cluster simulator — learners practice job submission, debugging, and optimization in an immersive virtual datacenter. The 2026 "SimWorld Studio" paper (auto environment generation for embodied agents) directly informs this.

## 📋 Recommendations

### Research
1. **Commission a systematic review** of VR learning outcomes (only 4 exist)
2. **Investigate individual differences** in VR learning (spatial ability, prior experience)
3. **Long-term retention studies** comparing VR vs. traditional learning
4. **AR for AI literacy** — novel research direction (0 papers, clear gap)

### Platform
1. **ki-kompetenz-training:** Prototype AR overlay for AI transparency prompts (point-of-work learning)
2. **HPC Courses:** Prototype VR cluster simulator (job submission, debugging in immersive environment)
3. **Both:** Incorporate multimodal assessment (gaze, interaction) — strong evidence base (237 papers)
4. **Both:** Design for accessibility from the start (71 papers on VR/AR accessibility)

### Corpus
1. Add `virtual-reality` and `extended-reality` as explicit taxonomy categories or subcategories (currently distributed across 10 categories)
2. Add targeted arXiv/OpenAlex queries for VR learning reviews and individual-differences research
3. Track "immersive learning" as an emerging theme in the landscape report
