## Taxonomy Frameworks to Cross-Reference

- Benjamin Bloom's Taxonomy → original 1956 framework (6 domains: Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Synthesis, Evaluation)
- Anderson & Krathwohl revision (2001) → active verbs, 4 knowledge dimensions (Factual, Conceptual, Procedural, Metacognitive)
- Dee Fink's Taxonomy of Significant Learning (2013) → 6 categories: Foundational Knowledge, Application, Integration, Human Dimension, Caring, Learning How to Learn
